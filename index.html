Before your first class

Frequently Asked Questions

Starting Jiu-Jitsu can feel a little intimidating. Here are answers to some of the most common questions from beginners.

Do I need previous experience?

No. Complete beginners are welcome, and the coach will guide you through the basics at a suitable pace.

Do I need to be fit before starting?

No. Your fitness will improve through training. You can work at your own pace and take breaks whenever needed.

What should I wear?

Wear comfortable sports clothing without zips, buttons or anything sharp. A fitted T-shirt or rashguard and shorts or leggings are ideal.

Do I need to bring any equipment?

No special equipment is needed for your first class. Bring water and wear clean training clothes.

Is the first class really free?

Yes. Your first class is free, so you can meet the team and see whether Jiu-Jitsu is right for you.

Can I train if I am nervous?

Absolutely. Most people feel nervous before their first class. The club is beginner-friendly and you will be shown what to do.
